Qingsong Yan is a 3D Reconstruction Engineer at XGRIDS. He holds a B.Eng. in Geomatics Engineering and M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing from the School of Geodesy and Geomatics at Wuhan University. His work focuses on 3D vision and photogrammetry, turning observations of the physical world into accurate, complete, and computable spatial representations.

His research spans multi-view and panoramic vision, camera pose and dense geometry, neural fields, 3D Gaussian Splatting, and large-scale scene reconstruction. At XGRIDS, he contributes to the development of spatial-intelligence products including LCC Studio and LCC Scan, bringing rigorous geometry and reliable systems into intuitive 3D experiences.
