颜青松毕业于武汉大学测绘学院，先后于 2016 年、2019 年和 2024 年获得测绘工程学士、摄影测量与遥感硕士及博士学位。现任 XGRIDS 三维重建工程师，并具备中级工程师（测绘）职称。他长期专注于三维视觉与摄影测量，探索如何从真实世界的观测中建立准确、完整且可计算的空间表达。

他的研究以几何一致性为核心，涵盖多视图与全景视觉、相机位姿与稠密几何、神经场与 3D Gaussian Splatting，以及大规模场景的表面重建、表示压缩与数据组织，已发表多篇学术论文。相比孤立地追逐单一指标，他更关心不同技术环节之间的内在联系，以及方法在复杂场景中是否经得起验证、复现与长期使用。

在 XGRIDS，他参与 LCC Studio 和 LCC Scan 等空间智能产品的研发，推动三维重建能力从研究原型走向真实体验。他相信，优秀的空间产品应同时具备严谨的几何基础、稳定高效的系统能力，以及克制而富有质感的视觉呈现；技术的价值，最终要在用户能够自然理解和使用的空间体验中完成。

## English

Qingsong Yan graduated from the School of Geodesy and Geomatics at Wuhan University, where he received his B.Eng. in Geomatics Engineering in 2016, and his M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing in 2019 and 2024. He is currently a 3D Reconstruction Engineer at XGRIDS and holds the professional title of Intermediate Engineer in Surveying and Mapping. His long-term work in 3D vision and photogrammetry asks how observations of the physical world can become accurate, complete, and computable spatial representations.

Grounded in geometric consistency, his research spans multi-view and panoramic vision, camera pose and dense geometry, neural fields and 3D Gaussian Splatting, as well as surface reconstruction, representation compression, and data organization for large-scale scenes. He has published multiple academic papers in these areas. Rather than optimizing isolated metrics, he is interested in the relationships among these components—and in whether a method remains verifiable, reproducible, and useful in complex real-world settings.

At XGRIDS, he contributes to the development of spatial-intelligence products including LCC Studio and LCC Scan, helping carry 3D reconstruction from research prototypes into real experiences. He believes that strong spatial products combine rigorous geometry, reliable and efficient systems, and restrained yet refined visual presentation. Technology finds its fullest value when it becomes a spatial experience people can intuitively understand and use.
