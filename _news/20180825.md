我所在的武汉大学“ICU More”团队凭借“带遮挡的开集人脸识别”项目，获得[第五届中国研究生智慧城市技术与创意设计大赛](https://cpipc.acge.org.cn/sysFile/downFile.do?fileId=b8d27bbd62ed47da8cb57528d00d366a)技术挑战赛三等奖。My team, ICU More at Wuhan University, received the Third Prize in the Smart City Technology Challenge of the [5th China Graduate Smart City Technology and Creative Design Competition](https://cpipc.acge.org.cn/sysFile/downFile.do?fileId=b8d27bbd62ed47da8cb57528d00d366a) for our project, “Occlusion-Aware Open-Set Face Recognition.”
